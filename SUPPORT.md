# Support

Need help with one of our projects? Here are the best ways to get support.

## Documentation

Check the documentation of the specific repository you're working with — most projects include a `README.md` and, where applicable, a wiki with guides and examples.

## Questions

If you have a question, [start a discussion](https://github.com/orgs/lengors-labs/discussions/new?category=q-a) in our community. Please search existing discussions and issues before creating a new one.

## Ideas and Suggestions

Want to share an idea or suggest an improvement? [Start an ideas discussion](https://github.com/orgs/lengors-labs/discussions/new?category=ideas) or [create a task](https://github.com/lengors-labs/.github/issues/new?template=create_task.yml).

## Bug Reports

If you've found a bug, please [report it](https://github.com/lengors-labs/.github/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml) and include as much detail as possible, such as steps to reproduce and any relevant logs or screenshots.

## Security

For security vulnerabilities, please **do not** open a public issue. Instead, follow the process described in our [Security Policy](./SECURITY.md).
